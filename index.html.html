Congregational Assessment Questionnaire
Preparing Trinity Congregation for 2027

Theme: Evaluating Our Progress, Strengthening Our Foundation and Planning for Greater Impact.

As the year draws to a close, it is important for Trinity Congregation to evaluate its activities, achievements, challenges and overall performance. This questionnaire is designed to obtain honest feedback from members concerning the spiritual life, leadership, administration, ministries, programmes and development of the congregation.

The objective is not to find fault with anyone but to identify areas of strength, weaknesses and opportunities for improvement as we prepare for 2027.

Instructions: Please answer all questions honestly. Select the option that best represents your opinion. Where necessary, provide additional comments. Responses will be treated confidentially and used for congregational development and planning.

PART ONE: SPIRITUAL GROWTH AND WORSHIP
1 How would you assess the overall spiritual growth of Trinity Congregation in the past year?
2 How satisfied are you with the quality of preaching, teaching and biblical instruction in the congregation?
3 Do the congregational prayer meetings, Bible studies, fasting programmes and other spiritual activities meet your spiritual needs?
4 How would you assess the quality of our Sunday worship services, including music, time management, participation and general organization?
PART TWO: LEADERSHIP AND ADMINISTRATION
5 How would you assess the effectiveness of the Minister, Presbyters and other congregational leaders in providing spiritual and administrative leadership?
6 Do you think congregational decisions, announcements and important information are communicated effectively to members?
7 Do you believe members have sufficient opportunities to contribute ideas, participate in decision-making and serve in the congregation?
PART THREE: MEMBERSHIP AND CHURCH DEVELOPMENT
8 How would you assess the level of love, unity, fellowship and mutual support among members of Trinity Congregation?
9 How effective are our evangelism, visitation and follow-up activities in attracting new members and retaining existing ones?
10 Are the needs of children, youth, young adults, women, men and the elderly adequately addressed through our congregational programmes?
11 How would you assess the performance of the various congregational committees and groups in carrying out their responsibilities?
PART FOUR: STEWARDSHIP, WELFARE AND INFRASTRUCTURE
12 How would you assess the congregation’s financial management, accountability and encouragement of members towards giving and stewardship?
13 What is your assessment of the congregation’s attention to members’ welfare, pastoral care, counselling, visitation and support during difficult times?
14 Which of the following areas should receive greater attention and investment in the coming year?
You may select up to three options.
PART FIVE: MINISTRIES, PROGRAMMES AND SPIRITUAL ACTIVITIES
15 How would you assess the contribution and effectiveness of Echoes of Trinity in enhancing worship, evangelism and the spiritual life of the congregation?
16 How would you assess the effectiveness of the Media Team in handling sound, projection, announcements, photography and other media responsibilities?
17 How helpful and effective is the live streaming of our congregational services in reaching members and people who cannot physically attend church?
18 How would you assess the Friday Prophetic Hour in terms of spiritual impact, prayer, participation and personal testimonies?
19 How would you assess the relevance and effectiveness of our Tuesday Bible Studies in promoting biblical understanding and spiritual maturity among members?
PART SIX: THE WAY FORWARD
20 What three major changes, improvements or new initiatives would you recommend to make Trinity Congregation a stronger, more vibrant and growing congregation in 2027?
SPECIAL PROGRAMMES AND MEMBER CARE
21 How would you assess the Monthly Day-Born Celebration programme in promoting fellowship, love, unity and appreciation of members celebrating their birthdays each month?
22 How would you assess the congregational Health Screening Programme in promoting the physical well-being and health awareness of members?

Thank you for taking the time to participate in this assessment. Your honest contribution is important to the future of Trinity Congregation.

Our desire is to build a spiritually vibrant, united, growing and financially sustainable congregation where every member feels valued and has an opportunity to contribute.

LET US EVALUATE OUR PROGRESS, LEARN FROM OUR EXPERIENCES AND MOVE FORWARD WITH A CLEAR VISION FOR 2027.
